# References

Every dataset, service, method, library, image, sound and tool used by Rotare, as required by the
NASA Space Apps 2026 submission guide ("list all data, code, text and images used, even open ones").
The collection script also writes the data references of each run to `data/<field>/manifesto.json`.

**How the data flows:** the collection pipeline (`coleta/`) downloads every source below into Parquet
tables in `data/`; the web app uses the Sorriso, MT tables bundled in `app/data/`, and any other field
is fetched live from Google Earth Engine and NASA POWER by the serverless API (`api/`). No value is
generated by AI: every number comes from these sources, processed by deterministic code.

field outside the bundled Sorriso region, plus daily temperatures from the NASA POWER API.

## Methods

| Method | Use | Citation |
| --- | --- | --- |

| FAO-56 extraterrestrial radiation and crop coefficients | ET0 and crop water demand | Allen, R.G., et al. (1998). Crop evapotranspiration. FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56. |
| Horn slope algorithm | Slope from a 3x3 SRTM window | Horn, B.K.P. (1981). Hill shading and the reflectance map. Proceedings of the IEEE, 69(1), 14-47. |
| ECOCROP suitability model | Indicative climate suitability per crop | Hijmans, R.J., et al. (2001). Plant Genetic Resources Newsletter, 127, 1-5. |
| USDA-NRCS Soil Conditioning Index | Structure of the relative soil index | USDA-NRCS. Soil Conditioning Index. |
| FAO-56 daily soil water balance | Root-zone depletion and the water-stress coefficient Ks for each crop window | Allen, R.G., Pereira, L.S., Raes, D., Smith, M. (1998). FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56, chapter 8. |
| FAO-33 yield response to water | Relative yield loss from water deficit: 1 − Yr = Ky (1 − ETa/ETc) | Doorenbos, J., Kassam, A.H. (1979). Yield response to water. FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 33. |
| Saxton and Rawls pedotransfer functions | Field capacity and wilting point (plant-available water) from SoilGrids sand, clay and carbon | Saxton, K.E., Rawls, W.J. (2006). Soil water characteristic estimates by texture and organic matter for hydrologic solutions. Soil Science Society of America Journal, 70(5), 1569-1578. |
| Crop residue and nutrient cycling | Orders of magnitude for straw and cycling in the soil index | Embrapa Cerrados. Sistema Plantio Direto no Cerrado (no-till system references). |
| ENSO season classification | Selection of the 6 strong El Niño analog seasons for El Niño Mode | NOAA CPC ONI thresholds (see Other data). |
| NDVI phenology rules | Inference of past crop cycles, planting and harvest windows and bare-soil periods | The team's own deterministic rules on the monthly NDVI curve, confirmed or corrected by the farmer. |
