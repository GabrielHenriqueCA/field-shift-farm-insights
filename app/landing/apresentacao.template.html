THE PROBLEMSORRISO, MT · 2014 TO 2024

THE CLIMATE HAS ALREADY CHANGED THE MATH.

In El Niño years,
the field feels it first.

Sorriso is one of Brazil's largest soybean producers. Even so, the second crop is usually chosen year by year, without looking at the field's own decade of history.

And a climate model alone can mislead: in 2015 it recorded 944 mm of rain. NASA's satellite measured 1,653 mm.

Why Sorriso? We chose Sorriso, Mato Grosso, as our demo region: Brazil's soybean heartland, where farmers decide the second crop every single year and El Niño hits hard. The method is not tied to it: Rotare's live mode works for any field, and we have already collected data for Iowa, Punjab, Pergamino and Eldoret.
